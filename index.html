BEFORE YOU ORDER

A few useful details.

Does the per-ton price include delivery?

No. Material, delivery and fuel are separate. Half truckload delivery is $145 for up to 11 tons; full truckload delivery is $185 for up to 22 tons. Fuel is $25 per delivery. Sales tax and any additional charges are not included in the online estimate.

Can you supply test reports for my project?

Ask for the available gradation and testing information for your selected material. Reports are specific to their source, sample and test date. Your project team should confirm suitability and any required approvals.

How do I convert cubic yards to tons?

The conversion depends on the material, moisture and density. Send us the material and volume you need so we can help estimate tonnage. Material is sold by the ton; final quantities depend on actual ticketed weight.

Can I request same-day delivery or several loads?

Yes, ask us about available scheduling and project pricing. Delivery is subject to availability. Same-day or rush service may cost extra, and multiple loads require a confirmed project quote.
